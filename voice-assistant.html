Case study 01 · Conversational AI

AI Voice Scheduling Assistant

Testing an AI voice assistant that handled customer conversations and appointment scheduling.

Objective

Reliable conversations, accurate scheduling, and business-rule compliance.

FocusVoice AI, prompt evaluation, business rules
TestingFunctional, regression, API, edge cases
ScopeIntent recognition, context, voice flow, CRM and notifications
ImpactIdentified production-impacting defects before release

Evaluation criteria

How I assess a voice AI response

Each response is evaluated through six quality lenses—balancing natural conversation with dependable business outcomes.

01 / UNDERSTANDING

Intent Recognition

Did the assistant correctly understand the user’s request?

02 / DIALOGUE

Conversation Flow

Was the dialogue natural, clear, and logical?

03 / MEMORY

Context Retention

Did the assistant remember previous information?

04 / ACCURACY

Business Rules

Were scheduling rules followed correctly?

05 / VOICE

Speech Understanding

Were names, dates, numbers, and addresses interpreted accurately?

06 / RESILIENCE

Recovery Handling

Could the assistant recover after interruptions or unclear input?

Test approach

Unlike traditional applications, user conversations were highly unpredictable.

  • Simulated interruptions, silence, ambiguity, invalid input, and context switching.
  • Validated booking availability, service eligibility, escalation, and confirmation flows.
  • Assessed intent recognition, speech understanding, recovery handling, and context retention.
Defects found: premature termination from incorrect intent detection, context loss after interruption, unavailable-information hallucinations, business-rule violations, and skipped confirmations.

💡 Lessons learned

A different testing mindset.

AI systems require a different testing mindset than traditional software. Success depends not only on verifying expected behavior, but also on understanding how the system behaves under uncertainty, ambiguity, and unexpected user interactions.

🏆 Key takeaways

Beyond the happy path.

This project strengthened my expertise in conversational AI testing by combining structured QA practices with real-world conversational analysis. It reinforced the importance of ensuring AI systems remain reliable, predictable, and trustworthy in production environments.
