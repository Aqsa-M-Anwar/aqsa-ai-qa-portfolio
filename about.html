AI Quality Engineer · 3+ years’ experience

Hi, I’m Aqsa Anwar. My journey started in software quality assurance; as AI products became more common, I was drawn to a different challenge: not just verifying features, but understanding how intelligent systems respond, reason, and sometimes fail.

Today I validate AI systems that interact with people through conversations, prompts, knowledge retrieval, and automation workflows.

Testing philosophy

Quality is the confidence we build before users ever interact with a product.

I look beyond happy paths—exploring interruptions, ambiguity, incomplete input, context loss, unexpected behaviour, business rules, and real-world edge cases. That is where the most valuable defects are found.

Response quality

Accuracy, relevance, clarity, tone, and grounded responses.

Conversation resilience

Context retention, recovery, interruptions, and user experience.

Reliable behaviour

Prompt adherence, business rules, safe boundaries, and consistency.

My approach

Look beyond the expected.

This mindset helps me discover issues that are not visible through traditional happy-path testing.

What happens if the user interrupts?

What if the information is incomplete?

Does the response follow business rules?

My goal
