LLM Response Evaluation

Evaluating accuracy, consistency, context retention, prompt adherence, and hallucination risk.

Objective

Deliver reliable responses under realistic, imperfect conversations.

FocusPrompt testing, response evaluation, hallucination detection
TestingFunctional, exploratory, negative, edge-case
ScenariosAmbiguous questions, long sessions, injection attempts, contradictions
CriteriaAccuracy, relevance, safety, consistency, instruction following

Approach

  • Designed prompt-validation scenarios and multi-turn conversations.
  • Compared repeated responses for consistency and unsupported claims.
  • Validated business rules, restricted responses, and safe acknowledgement of uncertainty.
Defects found: high-confidence hallucinations, loss of context in longer sessions, instruction failures, contradictory answers, and responses outside defined business boundaries.
