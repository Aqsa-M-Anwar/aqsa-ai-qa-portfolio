📊 Evaluation criteria

How I assess RAG quality

Each answer is evaluated across retrieval, grounding, relevance, and knowledge reliability.

01 / RETRIEVAL

Retrieval Accuracy

Was the correct document retrieved for the user’s query?

02 / GROUNDING

Grounding

Was the response based only on retrieved information?

03 / RELEVANCE

Response Relevance

Did the answer fully address the user’s request?

04 / SOURCING

Citation Quality

Were supporting sources correctly referenced when applicable?

05 / LIMITS

Missing Knowledge

Did the AI respond appropriately when information was unavailable?

Test approach

  • Tested the full retrieval pipeline, not just the final response.
  • Compared AI answers against source documentation and knowledge updates.
  • Validated follow-up behaviour, ambiguity handling, and missing-knowledge fallbacks.
Defects found: incorrect document selection, stale knowledge, fabricated responses without relevant content, partial answers, duplicate retrieval results, and refresh failures.

💡 Lessons learned

More than a language model.

A high-performing language model alone does not guarantee reliable AI responses. The quality of a RAG system depends equally on document retrieval, knowledge management, and response grounding. Testing must evaluate the complete information retrieval pipeline—not only generated answers.

🏆 Key takeaways

Trust starts with knowledge.

This project strengthened my expertise in validating AI systems that combine language models with external knowledge sources. It reinforced the importance of testing retrieval accuracy, response grounding, and knowledge consistency to deliver reliable, trustworthy AI experiences.
